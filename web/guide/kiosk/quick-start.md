Your unit uses SES Login to record the time you spend at the unit and what you
spent it on. You do that at the **kiosk**: the computer or tablet near the door
that says "Please enter or scan your SES ID".

## Signing in

1. Type your **SES ID** into the box, or scan your card if the kiosk has a
   scanner.
2. Press **Enter**, or the arrow button.

The kiosk shows your name and the time you signed in. That's it.

## Signing out

1. Enter your **SES ID** again.
2. Choose **what you did**, such as training, a meeting or a job.
3. Check the times and press **Submit**.

See [Signing out](signing-out.md) for choosing a category and fixing your times.

## If something's not right

- Been signed in since yesterday? See [Forgot to sign out](forgot-to-sign-out.md).
- Bringing a visitor? See [Guests](guests.md).
- Made a mistake after pressing **Submit**? See the [FAQ](faq.md).
