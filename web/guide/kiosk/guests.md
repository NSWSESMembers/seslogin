Visitors who aren't SES members can be signed in by name, if your unit has
turned guests on. You'll see a **Guest sign in / out** button under the SES ID
box.

## Signing a guest in

1. Press **Guest sign in / out**.
2. Enter the guest's **Name**, and a **Reason for visit** if you like.
3. Press **Sign in**.

## Signing a guest out

Press **Guest sign in / out** again. Under **Currently signed in guests**, press
**Sign out** next to their name.

Guests don't choose a category, and their time isn't counted in member reports.
