If you forget to sign out, your unit's administrators can email you a reminder
with a link to finish signing out. You can choose not to get those emails on
your member email settings page. It's separate from the kiosk and from the admin
dashboard: you don't need an admin account to use it.

## Opening your settings

There are two ways in:

- **From a reminder email.** Every reminder ends with a link to your email
  settings. It signs you in straight away, and works for 2 weeks. If it has
  expired, you're taken to the sign-in page with your email address already
  filled in.
- **By signing in.** Go to [Member email settings](/member/sign-in) (also linked
  at the foot of the SESLogin home page), enter your service email address and
  press **Send code**. Enter the 6-digit code we email you, and press **Sign
  in**. The code works for 10 minutes.

> [!NOTE]
> Use the email address SES holds for you, usually your service email. If no
> code arrives, ask your unit to check the email address SES has for you.

## Turning reminders off or on

Untick **Remind me when I forget to sign out** to stop the reminders, or tick it
to get them again. Your change is saved as soon as you make it.

Turning reminders off doesn't stop your unit asking you to check an entry that
already has a finish time: those emails still come, and still link to your
settings.

When you're done, press **Sign out**, especially on a shared computer.
