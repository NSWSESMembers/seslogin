If your unit has badges turned on, signing in and out earns you badges. When you
earn one, the kiosk shows it straight after your scan. This page shows every
badge and what earns it.

## How badges work

- **Your unit has to take part.** Badges count when a unit you belong to, the unit
  you sign in at and that unit's kiosk all have badges turned on. If you don't see
  any badges, ask your unit's administrators.
- **Most badges are earned at each unit.** Counts are kept separately for each
  unit you sign in at. So 25 sign-ins means 25 at one unit, and you can earn the
  same badge again at another unit. The badges that count across every unit say so.
- **Counting starts when your unit turns badges on.** Sign-ins from before then
  don't count towards the running totals (sign-ins, sign-outs, hours and
  categories). The streak, deployment and service badges are worked out from
  your actual sign-in history (see below), so earlier sign-ins can count for those.
- **Some badges arrive overnight.** Streaks, deployments and years of service are
  checked once a night. A badge earned that way is shown the next time you scan
  at a kiosk, even if the sign-ins that earned it were a while ago.
- **Sign-outs count by category.** Category badges count the category you choose
  when you [sign out](signing-out.md). Choosing the right category gets you the
  right badges.
- **Days and dates are Sydney time.** A sign-in belongs to the day it started.

Each badge has a tier: **Starter**, **Bronze**, **Silver** and **Gold** go from
easiest to hardest. **Passport Stamps** are for visiting other units, and
**Wildcard** badges are surprises for unusual moments.

counts.
