## I chose the wrong category, or the wrong times

Once you've pressed **Submit**, the kiosk can't change it. Ask your unit's
administrators: they can [correct any entry](../admin/activity.md#correcting-an-entry).

## I signed in by mistake

Enter your SES ID again to sign out, and ask your unit's administrators to
delete the entry if it shouldn't count.

## The kiosk is showing a QR code

The kiosk needs setting up again, usually because it's been switched off for a
while. Let your unit's administrators know. They can bring it back in a minute
(see [When a kiosk expires](../admin/kiosks.md#when-a-kiosk-expires)).

## Can I use another unit's kiosk?

Yes. See [Signing in at another unit](signing-in.md#signing-in-at-another-unit).

## Where can I see my hours?

Your unit's administrators can see everyone's activity and run reports. Ask them
for yours.
