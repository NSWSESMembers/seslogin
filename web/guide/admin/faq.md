## Can a member from another unit sign in at our kiosk?

Yes. Any member of any unit can sign in and out at any kiosk. Their time is
recorded in your unit's activity, with their home unit shown under their name
("Visiting from" for someone who doesn't belong to your unit), and also appears
in their own history.

## People keep signing in as the wrong person

That's almost always a mistyped SES ID that happens to belong to someone at
another unit. Turn on [Confirm visitors](kiosks.md#options) for the kiosk: it
then asks anyone who isn't a member of your unit to confirm their name before signing them in.

## Can we have more than one kiosk?

Yes, as many as you like. Members don't need to sign out at the same kiosk they
signed in at: it's counted as one entry at your unit.

## Can I tell which kiosk someone used?

Yes. Each entry records the kiosk the member signed in at and the one they signed
out at. In the [activity list](activity.md#seeing-which-kiosk-was-used), hover
over (or tap) an entry's start or end time to see the kiosk's name. The
[spreadsheet download](reports.md#downloading-a-spreadsheet) has a column for
each.

## What if someone signs in at one unit and out at another?

That makes two sign-ins, because an entry can't span units. The member is then
signed in at both. Entering their SES ID at either unit signs them out there.

## A member forgot to sign out. What do I do?

Find them under **Activity → Incomplete Periods**, press **Edit**, set the right
end time and press **Save**. See [Correcting an entry](activity.md#correcting-an-entry).
If they come back to the kiosk more than 12 hours later, it offers to fix the
entry for them.

## A member isn't in our list

If your unit's members come from SES, a new member appears within a day of being
added there. In the meantime — or if your unit isn't synced — an Admin can
[add them](members.md#adding-a-member).

## A member has an orange dot, or has disappeared

An orange dot means the member is no longer in your unit's list in SES. They're
removed from SESLogin after about a week unless they reappear in SES. Their past
activity is kept. See [When members leave your unit](members.md#when-members-leave-your-unit).

## Can our time go into Beacon NITCs automatically?

Yes, once NITC export is turned on for your unit. See [NITC export](nitc.md).

## Our kiosk stopped working

If it shows a QR code, it has expired, usually after two weeks switched off.
Press **Reactivate** next to it on the **Kiosks** page — within two weeks, or it
has to be set up again. See [When a kiosk expires](kiosks.md#when-a-kiosk-expires).

If it says **This computer's clock is wrong**, the kiosk is still set up — don't
scan anything. Fix the computer's date and time and the kiosk carries on by
itself within a minute. See [If the kiosk says its clock is wrong](kiosks.md#if-the-kiosk-says-its-clock-is-wrong).

## I didn't get my sign-in code

Check your junk mail and that you used the address your account was set up with,
then press **Start over**. Codes expire after 10 minutes. A
[passkey](signing-in.md#passkeys) saves waiting for codes.

## How do I get help?

Use **Help/Feedback?** at the bottom of any admin page, or email
[support@seslogin.com](mailto:support@seslogin.com).
