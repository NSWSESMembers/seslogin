Every administrator account has a role at each unit it can see.

## Admin and Read only

| You can…                             | Admin | Read only |
| ------------------------------------ | :---: | :-------: |
| See the dashboard, activity, reports |  Yes  |    Yes    |
| See members and the audit log        |  Yes  |    Yes    |
| Download spreadsheets                |  Yes  |    Yes    |
| Get the daily email summary          |  Yes  |    Yes    |
| Add, edit or delete activity entries |  Yes  |    No     |
| Add, edit or delete members          |  Yes  |    No     |
| Set up, change or remove kiosks      |  Yes  |    No     |

Read only users see a notice on pages they can't change, and the buttons for
changes are hidden.

Unit-wide settings, such as the unit's name and [NITC export](nitc.md), are
managed by the SES Login maintainers. Ask them for changes.

Someone can hold different roles at different units — for example, Admin at
their own unit and Read only at a neighbouring one.

## Getting access

Accounts are created by the SES Login maintainers. To get access for yourself or
someone at your unit, use **Help/Feedback?** at the bottom of any admin page, or
email [support@seslogin.com](mailto:support@seslogin.com), with:

- the unit name;
- each person's email address;
- whether each should be **Admin** or **Read only**.

We suggest no more than three Admins per unit to start with.

## Administrators and members are separate

Your administrator account is not the same as your member record. You can't sign
in at a kiosk with your email — use your SES ID like any other member. If you're
not in your unit's [member list](members.md), you can add yourself.
