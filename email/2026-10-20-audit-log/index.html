New features: Audit log and Read only role

See who changed what, and when

SES Activity now keeps an Audit log for your unit: a record of every change made, newest first. Activity entries added, edited or deleted, members added or changed, kiosks set up or removed — it's all there, so you never have to wonder how an entry ended up the way it is.

  • Who, what and when. Each row shows when the change was made, who made it, what they did (such as Created, Updated or Deleted) and what it was made to.
  • Exactly what changed. Press Details on any row to see which fields changed, from what to what — for example, a sign-out time that was corrected.
  • Automatic changes too. Changes made by SES Activity itself, such as the daily update of your member list from SES, show what made them instead of a person's email address.
  • Filter to what you need. Use Show to pick one or more kinds of change, such as only activity entries or only kiosks.

The audit log, newest change first. Names are examples.

To open it, log in to SES Activity and choose Audit log from the menu.

Open the audit log

Also new: the Read only role

Want to give someone access to your unit without letting them change anything? There's now a Read only role alongside Admin. A Read only user can see the dashboard, activity, reports, members and the audit log, download spreadsheets and get the daily email summary, but can't add, edit or delete anything. It suits anyone who needs to keep an eye on your unit's activity without managing it.

Want us to add someone, as an Admin or Read only? Reply to this email, or submit a ticket using the Help/Feedback? link at the bottom of any page on the admin site. Let us know the unit, each person's email address, and whether they should be Admin or Read only.

Questions, problems or feedback? Just reply to this email. Your reply opens a support ticket with the SES Activity team, so you can report an issue, tell us what you think of a change, or ask for help, and we'll get back to you.
