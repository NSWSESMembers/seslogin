New: iPad kiosk improvements

A much better iPad kiosk

SES Activity kiosks now work significantly better on an iPad:

  • Full screen. Step-by-step instructions set the kiosk up as a Home Screen app, with no browser toolbars.
  • Stays awake for longer. The screen should dim and lock much less often between sign-ins.
  • Made for touch. A large on-screen number pad replaces the iPad's keyboard.
  • Fits smaller screens. Category buttons resize to fit, and pop-ups scroll. This helps any kiosk with a small screen, not just iPads.

Action needed: iPad kiosks

Is your iPad kiosk running in Safari?

Check the top of the kiosk's screen. If Safari's address bar is showing, the kiosk needs setting up again to run full screen and stay awake for longer.

If you can see Safari's address bar:

  1. On your Kiosks page, note the kiosk's name and settings, then click Delete.
  2. The iPad then shows the new Add this kiosk to the Home Screen instructions (reload the page if they haven't appeared after a minute). Follow them.
  3. Open seslogin from the new Home Screen icon, scan its QR code with your phone, and give the kiosk back its name and settings.

Home Screen setup

Open the kiosk page in Safari on an iPad and it now shows these instructions before you enroll. A Home Screen kiosk fills the screen and is better at staying awake. Because it keeps its own storage, apart from Safari, you only need to enroll it once.

What an iPad shows when the kiosk page is opened in Safari.

Stays unlocked for longer

The kiosk now asks the iPad to keep its screen on, so it should dim and lock itself much less often between sign-ins, though it may still happen now and then. On a Home Screen kiosk this needs iPadOS 18.4 or later, so check Settings > General for updates, and keep the iPad plugged in.

Made for the touchscreen

On an iPad, tapping the member ID field opens a large number pad, and the iPad's keyboard no longer covers half the screen.
