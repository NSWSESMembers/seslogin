New feature: Who's here

See who's here at a glance

Your sign-in kiosks can now show a list of who's currently signed in at your unit, right beside the sign-in screen. Set the kiosk's Who's here option to Always visible and the list stays on screen permanently, so anyone walking past can see who's in without pressing anything.

  • Everyone signed in at your unit. The list covers sign-ins from all of your unit's kiosks, not just the one it's showing on.
  • Updates in real time. In most cases the list changes the moment someone signs in or out, with no refresh needed.
  • Shows how long each member has been signed in. Times start green and turn yellow, orange and then red as a sign-in passes 6, 8, 10 and 12 hours. That makes it great for fatigue management: you can see at a glance who has been on for a long stretch and may need a break or to head home.

A kiosk with Who's here set to Always visible. Names are examples.

How to turn it on

  1. Log in to SES Activity and go to Kiosks.
  2. Click Edit next to the kiosk.
  3. Under Who's here, select Always visible.
  4. Click Save.

A kiosk that's switched on picks up the change straight away; one that's off gets it the next time it connects. The option is set separately for each kiosk and appears for kiosks in Scan mode.

Prefer to keep the screen clear? Choose Button instead, which adds a button to the sign-in screen that opens the list when needed.
